“Warmup” can mean two different things: gradually introducing legitimate email traffic, or using a tool that exchanges automated messages with a network of inboxes. Those approaches should not be treated as interchangeable evidence of reputation.

## Start with readiness

Check account permissions, sender identity, authentication, reply routing, and opt-out handling. Review the intended use against the mailbox and sending providers’ policies. Follow [Google’s sender requirements](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126?hl=en) where applicable.

Before changing DMARC enforcement, identify all legitimate senders and review alignment. A calendar-based rule such as “switch to reject after seven days” can disrupt valid mail if another system has not been configured.

## Avoid a guaranteed warmup timetable

There is no universal two-, four-, or six-week period after which a new account is ready for any volume. Sending history, audience relevance, provider restrictions, and observed delivery all matter. Agree an initial review date and extend preparation when the evidence calls for it.

If a vendor recommends automated warmup, ask what it actually measures and whether that activity complies with your providers’ policies. Simulated opens or replies do not demonstrate real prospect interest. A high warmup score is not a promise of inbox placement.

## Run a controlled pilot

Prepare a small, relevant audience with verified work-contact details and appropriate suppression checks. Start at a conservative operating volume. Review delivery errors, complaints, replies, and available reputation information before increasing sends. Stop activity when an account restriction or material delivery problem needs investigation.

Record the date, audience, message variant, attempted sends, failures, and replies. Without that context, a sudden percentage change is difficult to diagnose.

## Respond to problems by investigating

Check authentication changes, provider notices, stale data, misleading copy, and recent volume changes. Adjust the relevant cause rather than automatically doubling automated warmup. Recovery time is uncertain.

Open rates can be distorted by privacy features and automated image fetching. Use them cautiously alongside delivery and conversation metrics. Keep the launch schedule in your proposal consistent with the actual readiness process.
