Intent data suggests that an account may be investigating a topic. It does not prove that a particular person has budget, authority, or plans to purchase.

## Separate the types of evidence

An explicit demo request provides different evidence from a pricing-page visit. Third-party research activity is different again: it may be aggregated, inferred, or associated with an account rather than a named person.

Keep those distinctions in the CRM. Email opens and clicks can be affected by privacy features and security scanners, so they should not be treated as unquestionable human interest.

## Evaluate the source

Ask the provider what activity generates a signal, how an account is matched, how often the data refreshes, and which markets are covered. Understand permitted use and the information that is actually available.

Vendors to investigate include [Bombora](https://bombora.com/), [6sense](https://6sense.com/), [G2](https://www.g2.com/), and [Common Room](https://www.commonroom.io/). These are starting points for evaluation, not an independently tested ranking. Obtain current quotes and sample coverage for your own audience rather than relying on unsourced price ranges.

## Prioritize by fit and context

Combine topic relevance, recency, account fit, and existing conversations. An operational score can help route work, but validate it against outcomes. There is no universal 48-hour buying window or fixed date after which every signal becomes worthless.

For an account with several possible stakeholders, identify roles and coordinate introductions. Do not send the same message to everyone or imply surveillance of a person’s browsing.

## Measure incremental usefulness

Compare similar account groups with and without the signal layer. Define positive replies, meetings, and opportunities, and record data and outreach costs. Separate observed association from evidence of causation.

Begin with the signals you already have permission to use. Add another source only when it addresses a specific coverage or prioritization gap and its cost can be assessed against the results.
