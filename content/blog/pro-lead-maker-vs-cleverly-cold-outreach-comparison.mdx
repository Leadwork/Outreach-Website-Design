## Start with the same brief

Give both providers the audience, offer, target regions, existing systems, and desired channels. Ask for the same definitions of unique contacts, messages, follow-ups, qualified replies, and meetings. Otherwise two monthly prices may describe different amounts of work.

Do not assume a provider’s team size determines whether its delivery is personalized. Ask who will manage your account, how copy is researched and approved, and how results are reviewed.

## Questions that make the comparison useful

Confirm included licenses, domain and mailbox ownership, activity limits, support hours, reporting, setup work, and cancellation notice. Ask whether Sales Navigator or additional data is a separate expense. Request the exact conditions of any meeting or replacement promise.

Review genuine case studies with audience, dates, campaign scope, and metric definitions. Distinguish bookings from attendance and closed revenue. Ask how the provider handles account restrictions and opts recipients out across channels.

## Decide from the proposal

Our monthly outreach plans have 30 days’ cancellation notice and no promised meeting count. We provide a defined scope with direct communication from our team. Confirm Cleverly’s corresponding terms in its current proposal rather than relying on older third-party comparisons.

Choose the provider whose documented scope, evidence, communication, and operating approach fit your needs.
