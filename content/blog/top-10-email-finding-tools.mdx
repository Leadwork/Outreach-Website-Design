An email finder is useful only if its records match the people you need and its permitted use fits your workflow. A large database or a vendor accuracy claim is not enough to establish that.

## A shortlist for evaluation

Explore [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/), [Hunter](https://hunter.io/), [Snov.io](https://snov.io/), [RocketReach](https://rocketreach.co/), [FindThatLead](https://findthatlead.com/), [Skrapp](https://skrapp.io/), [GetProspect](https://getprospect.com/), [Lusha](https://www.lusha.com/), [ContactOut](https://contactout.com/), and [Voila Norbert](https://www.voilanorbert.com/).

This is a research shortlist, not an accuracy ranking. Product availability, features, credits, prices, and terms change. Confirm details directly with the provider for the intended use and billing period.

## Compare on your own audience

Use a representative sample across your target regions, company sizes, and roles. Define the required fields before evaluating results. Track records found, records matching the intended person, verification classifications, duplicates, and unresolved cases separately.

Measure cost per usable record after quality review. A cheap lookup is not necessarily a cheap qualified contact, and a second verifier does not make uncertainty disappear.

## Inspect the operating details

Ask about provenance, collection dates, export rights, suppression controls, and deletion requests. Check how credits are charged, whether unknown results consume them, and whether integrations require a higher plan. Distinguish subscription, annual commitment, and pay-as-you-go terms.

Evaluate LinkedIn-related extensions or exports against the platform’s rules rather than assuming availability means authorization. Prefer appropriate business-contact information; access to personal contact details does not establish permission to use them for outreach.

## Maintain quality after purchase

Deduplicate and apply customer and opt-out exclusions before import. Verify time-sensitive details near the planned use date and flag catch-all results separately. Keep the source and verification date so a later problem can be investigated.
